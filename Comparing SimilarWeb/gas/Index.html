About

SimilarWeb Comparing — dashboard for managing client/competitor comparison data from SimilarWeb via Browse AI robots. Data is stored in Supabase PostgreSQL. Tasks are processed by n8n workflow.

Tables: clients, competitors, similarweb_data, task_queue, error_logs
Metrics: monthly_visits, unique_visitors, visit_duration, pages_per_visit, bounce_rate, visits_per_visitor, deduplicated_audience, page_views, direct, organic_search, paid_search, display_ads, social, email, ai_traffic
